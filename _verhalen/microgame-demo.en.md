  In a few minutes you experience how a MicroGame makes abstract knowledge practiseable.

  - Short, playable learning moments
  - Immediate feedback on your choice
  - Knowledge tied to a recognisable situation
uitdaging: >-
  Theory often stays on the page. You read it, nod, and move on — until the moment you
  actually need it. Then knowing turns out to be different from being able to do it.
aanpak: >-
  A MicroGame puts you in a small situation where you have to choose. No long briefing
  first. You experience the principle while you play, with immediate feedback at every step.
route: >-
  This first demo is based on Basics of Refrigeration: recognising components, placing the
  cycle, and applying theory in a conversation. That is how a technical concept becomes playable.
resultaat: >-
  You get a first impression of how MicroGames work — and whether this form fits your
  learning goal. If you want to see it live, we are happy to walk through it together.
inzicht: >-
  Learning sticks better when you do it. A demo is not a product tour, but a short moment
  of practice.
