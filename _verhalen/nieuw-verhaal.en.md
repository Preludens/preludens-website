uitdaging: >-
  Demand for heat pumps, air conditioning and sustainable cooling systems is growing fast.
  At the same time, the theory behind refrigeration remains a barrier for many installation technicians.
  Concepts such as the mechanical refrigeration cycle, superheat, subcooling and the
  h-log-P diagram are essential — but hard to grasp from a book or traditional e-learning alone.
ontwerpvraag: >-
  How do we make complex refrigeration knowledge accessible, interactive and applicable —
  so participants not only know how the refrigeration cycle works, but also recognise it in their work?
aanpak: >-
  Together with Climatools BV, 21CC Education and Studium we developed the Basic Refrigeration
  (BRL200) programme. It includes 12 short MicroGames: participants get an explanation,
  make choices, analyse situations and receive immediate feedback. Learning feels like practice,
  not like working through theory.
route: >-
  The MicroGames cover how components work, thermodynamic principles, safety, regulations
  and practical dilemmas. Participants practise step by step: first the concept, then the choice,
  then the application in a recognisable work situation.
ervaring: >-
  Instead of only reading about superheat or the h-log-P diagram, participants see what their
  choices do. The theory becomes visible and discussable, in a safe practice setting.
resultaat: >-
  The programme comprises 12 interactive MicroGames within Basic Refrigeration (BRL200).
  Abstract theory is now visible, practical and interactive — closer to the daily work
  of the installation technician.
inzicht: >-
  Game-based learning helps in technical education when the theory returns in the work itself.
  This project was developed with Climatools BV, 21CC Education, Studium and Preludens.
